    Why "show your work"

    A calculator that tells you how it read you.

    • Read as. Every answer comes with your calculation written back with all its hidden brackets, so -2^2 shows −(2^2) before it says −4. Whatever the calculator added is marked in yellow.
    • The other reading. Where calculators genuinely disagree, like 6/2(1+2), the screen tells you what the other reading would give.
    • Degrees unless you say so. The screen always shows DEG or RAD, and the reading writes the ° it assumed into every sine.
    • The real number underneath. Answers show 12 significant figures; when that rounds, a ≈ appears and the exact double is printed below.
