"Exact" means the number is fixed by definition, not measured. G, me and mp are measured; the calculator stores CODATA 2018 values and does not carry their uncertainty. Values are listed as the calculator stores them, as 64-bit doubles. Sources: BIPM, SI defining constants; NIST, CODATA values.
