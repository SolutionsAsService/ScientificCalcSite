Arithmetic

+ − × ÷

Add, subtract, multiply, divide. × and ÷ come before + and −.

  • 1+2*31 + (added opening bracket) (2 × 3(added closing bracket) )= 7
  • 10/410 ÷ 4= 2.5

x^y

Power. Right to left, so 2^3^2 is 2^(3^2). It comes before a leading minus.

  • 2^102^10= 1024
  • 2^3^22^(added opening bracket) (3^2(added closing bracket) )= 512
  • -2^2−(added opening bracket) (2^2(added closing bracket) )= −4

n!

Factorial of a whole number from 0 to 170.

  • 5!5!= 120
  • 0!0!= 1

x%

Percent: divides by 100.

  • 200*15%200 × (added opening bracket) (15%(added closing bracket) )= 30

( )

Brackets. A number next to a bracket, a constant or a root multiplies.

  • 2(3+4)2(added times) × (3 + 4)= 14
  • 2pi2(added times) × π= 6.28318530718

Counting and rounding

nCr(n, r)

Combinations: ways to choose r from n. Above 2⁵³ (about 9 × 10¹⁵) results are floating-point approximations.

  • nCr(52,5)nCr(52, 5)= 2598960

nPr(n, r)

Permutations: ordered choices of r from n.

  • nPr(5,2)nPr(5, 2)= 20

mod(a, b)

Remainder, always 0 or positive for positive b.

  • mod(-7,3)mod(−7, 3)= 2

abs sign floor ceil round

Absolute value, sign, and rounding. round sends halves away from zero.

  • round(2.5)round(2.5)= 3
  • round(-2.5)round(−2.5)= −3
